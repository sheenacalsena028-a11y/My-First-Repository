## My Favorite Movie

**Movie Title:** The Notebook

**Genre:** Romance and Drama

**Description:**  
The Notebook is a romantic drama about Noah and Allie, two young people who fall in love despite coming from different backgrounds. Their relationship faces many challenges, but their love remains meaningful throughout the years.

**Why I Like It:**  
I like this movie because it shows the importance of true love, patience, and commitment. It reminds me that genuine love can remain strong even when people face difficult situations.
